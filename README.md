# TARS
Tied observations affect the interpretation and estimation of rank-based spectra. We define the target through the distributional transform and obtain a deterministic estimator by conditioning the corresponding randomized estimator on the observations. The estimator combines a Bartlett spectrum of fractional indicator scores with a zero-lag covariance correction. It is positive semidefinite, integrates to the prescribed quantile-indicator covariance, and satisfies an exact conditional squared-error decomposition. Under unweighted integrated squared error, the correction removes a separate nonnegative term for every sample. For geometrically mixing processes on a fixed finite support, we establish uniform consistency and a pointwise Gaussian limit. Estimating the marginal distribution has a smaller asymptotic effect than the spectral fluctuation, including at cumulative category probabilities. Simulations give lower mean integrated squared error than the feasible comparators across all examined models, sample sizes, and bandwidths, with larger gains under coarser categorization. Applications to hourly cloud cover and recorded oxygen saturation show that conventional rank assignments can yield zero spectra or substantial normalization errors. In both simulations and applications, the mean squared distance from averages of $B$ randomized spectra to the proposed estimator decreases at the predicted $1/B$ rate. The fractional-score formula evaluates this conditional mean directly.
